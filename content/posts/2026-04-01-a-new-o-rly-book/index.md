## Description
Another one parody of the covers of a well-known book publisher.

The work continues the folk art series of [O RLY book covers](https://boyter.org/2016/04/collection-orly-book-covers/).

The endangered species on the cover of this book is Brainlet, a member of [Wojak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wojak)'s kin.
